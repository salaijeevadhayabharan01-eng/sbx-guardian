Defense in Depth
7 layers of security

Every piece of data passes through multiple security layers from the moment it enters SBX Guardian.

1
TLS Encryption in Transit
All data transmitted to SBX Guardian uses TLS 1.2 or higher. Webhook payloads, API calls, and dashboard traffic are all encrypted in transit.
2
Webhook Signature Verification
Every webhook has a unique 32-byte cryptographic secret. Requests without valid signatures are rejected before processing.
3
JWT Authentication
All API access requires a signed JSON Web Token. Tokens expire after 7 days and contain organizational scope — you can only access your own data.
4
Multi-tenant Data Isolation
Every database query is scoped by organization ID at the application layer. No cross-tenant data access is architecturally possible.
5
SHA-256 Hash Chain Integrity
Every event is cryptographically chained to the previous event. Any attempt to modify, delete, or insert a record breaks the chain and is detected immediately.
6
AES-256-GCM Encryption at Rest
Sensitive data fields are encrypted using AES-256 in GCM mode with unique initialization vectors per record. Encryption keys are environment-isolated.
7
Audit Trail
Every action in the platform is logged with timestamp, user identity, IP address, and operation. Audit logs are tamper-evident and accessible to account admins.

Data Practices
Your data. Your rules.
🚫
We never sell your data
Your compliance data, event logs, and reports are never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than providing the service.
🧠
We never train AI on your data
Your operational data is never used to train AI models — ours or third parties'. AI-generated reports use your data only to produce the report.
📦
Data portability always
Export all your data in JSON format at any time from Settings → GDPR Export. No lock-in. Your data leaves with you if you cancel.
🗑
Right to erasure
Delete your account and all associated data at any time. Data is anonymized within 30 days and permanently deleted within 90 days.
⏱
Minimum 6-month retention
Event logs are retained for at least 6 months as required by EU AI Act Article 12. You can configure longer retention for your compliance needs.
🔔
72-hour breach notification
If a data breach affects your organization's data, we notify you within 72 hours as required by GDPR Article 33.
