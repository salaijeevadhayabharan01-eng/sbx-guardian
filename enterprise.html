Common Questions
Enterprise FAQ
Do you have SOC 2 Type II certification?
SOC 2 Type II is currently in preparation with an audit planned for Q3 2026. We follow SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria as our design standard and can provide detailed security controls documentation. Enterprise clients can receive a pre-audit security report under NDA.
Can we get a custom Data Processing Agreement?
Yes. Enterprise clients receive custom DPA negotiation as standard. Our base DPA is GDPR Article 28 compliant. For enterprise, we work with your legal team to address specific requirements including sub-processor approval workflows, audit rights, and jurisdiction-specific clauses.
Where is our data stored? Can EU data stay in the EU?
Currently hosted on Render.com infrastructure. EU data residency is available as an enterprise option — contact us to discuss private cloud or EU-region deployment. Standard SCCs cover international transfers for all plans.
Can we deploy on our own infrastructure?
On-premise and private cloud deployment is available for enterprise clients. This requires a custom engagement — contact our enterprise team to discuss requirements and timeline.
How do we integrate with our existing systems?
SBX Guardian connects via webhook (any HTTP-capable system) and REST API (full OpenAPI 3.0 documentation). Pre-built connectors exist for Azure IoT Hub, AWS IoT Core, Siemens MindSphere, ROS/ROS2, and MQTT. Custom integrations are available for enterprise clients.
How long does procurement typically take?
For SME/Professional plans: procurement typically completes in 1-2 weeks with our standard legal documents. For enterprise: 4-12 weeks is typical, depending on your organization's vendor approval process. We actively support procurement by providing security questionnaire responses, executive briefings, and legal documentation.
