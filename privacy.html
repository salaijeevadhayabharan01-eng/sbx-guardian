Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026 · GDPR Compliant
Your data is yours. We process your data only to provide the SBX Guardian service. We never sell your data. We never share it with third parties for advertising. Your compliance data stays isolated to your organization.

SBX Guardian operates as the Data Controller for account and usage data, and as a Data Processor for the compliance and event data you submit through the platform.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to provide the Service
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Security monitoring, fraud prevention
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Compliance with EU AI Act, GDPR requirements
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Marketing emails (you can withdraw at any time)

4. Data Isolation and Security

Every organization's data is completely isolated at the database level. Your event logs, compliance data, and reports are never accessible to other organizations. We enforce this through:

  • Row-level security — every database query filters by organization ID
  • AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data at rest
  • SHA-256 hash chains proving log integrity
  • JWT tokens scoped to your organization
  • Webhook secrets unique to your account

5. Data Sharing

We share your data with:

  • Render.com — our hosting provider (EU/US data centers)
  • Groq / Google Gemini — AI providers for report generation (your data is sent to generate reports but not stored by them for training)
  • Nodemailer/Gmail SMTP — for sending alert emails you configure

We do NOT share your data with law enforcement except when legally required. We do NOT sell your data. We do NOT use your compliance data to train AI models.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Export all your data via Settings → GDPR Export
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data in your account settings
  • Erasure: Delete your account and data via Settings → Delete Account
  • Portability: Download your data in JSON format
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

To exercise rights: privacy@sbxguardian.com — we respond within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

Event logs are retained for a minimum of 6 months to meet EU AI Act Article 12 requirements. You may configure longer retention periods. Upon account deletion, data is anonymized within 30 days and permanently deleted within 90 days, except where legal obligations require longer retention.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in the United States (Render.com servers). We ensure adequate protection through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and data processing agreements with all sub-processors.

9. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for authentication (session tokens). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No third-party analytics are embedded in the dashboard.

10. Children's Privacy

SBX Guardian is a B2B service not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be communicated by email 14 days before taking effect.

12. Contact and Complaints

Privacy inquiries: privacy@sbxguardian.com

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority if you believe we have mishandled your data.
