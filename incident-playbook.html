Incident Response

Robot incident.
First 72 hours.

When an industrial AI system causes an injury, near-miss, or significant incident — you have 72 hours before legal exposure multiplies. This is your exact step-by-step playbook, written against EU AI Act legal requirements.

0
Minutes 0–15 · Immediate
Scene Security & Life Safety
1
Stop the system immediately
Activate emergency stop. Do NOT attempt to restart. Do NOT move the robot. The physical state of the system at the moment of incident is forensic evidence.
DO
Press E-stop. Secure perimeter. Call emergency services if injuries.
DO NOT
Restart the system. Move the robot. Delete any logs. Touch control interfaces.
2
Preserve the digital evidence
SBX Guardian's logs are automatically preserved and hash-chained. DO NOT manually clear system logs, modify software, or update firmware until legal counsel has reviewed. Your logs are your defense.
EU AI Act Art. 12 — Log preservation is legally mandatory. Deletion is obstruction.
3
Document with photos/video immediately
Photograph the scene from multiple angles before anything is moved. Record the robot's final position, any physical damage, and the environment. Timestamp everything. This is admissible evidence.
Emergency stop activated
Area secured / access restricted
Emergency services called (if injury)
Photos/video of scene taken
SBX Guardian log preservation confirmed
Time of incident recorded precisely
1
Hours 1–4 · Urgent
Internal Notifications
Notify Immediately
Legal Counsel / In-house Lawyer
Do not speak to insurers or regulators without legal present.
Notify Immediately
CEO / C-Suite
Board may need to be informed. Regulatory notifications require executive sign-off.
Notify Immediately
Head of Safety / EHS Manager
Responsible for regulatory notifications and investigation leadership.
Notify Immediately
IT / Data Protection Officer
Must confirm log preservation and access restriction to system.
4
Establish an Incident Response Team
Assign specific roles immediately: Incident Commander (EHS Lead), Legal Coordinator, Technical Investigator, Communications Lead, Evidence Custodian. One person owns each role. No role ambiguity.
Legal counsel notified and engaged
C-suite and board (if required) informed
Incident response team assembled
Roles assigned and documented
Communications hold placed (no public statements yet)
2
Hours 4–24 · Urgent
Evidence Collection & Regulatory Assessment
Export from SBX Guardian Immediately
  • Full audit export (Dashboard → Audit → Export) — generates certified audit package with reference number
  • Hash chain verification certificate — proves logs have not been tampered with
  • System health timeline — last 24 hours of all sensor and status data
  • Anomaly detection log — any warnings before the incident
  • Operator activity log — who interacted with the system and when
  • AI decision log — every autonomous decision made in the 2 hours before incident
5
Determine regulatory notification obligations
Within 24 hours, your legal team must determine: (1) Is this notifiable under EU AI Act Article 73 (serious incidents)? (2) Does it trigger Machinery Directive reporting? (3) Is there a GDPR breach component? (4) Do national labor law reporting obligations apply? Different incidents have different notification deadlines.
EU AI Act Art. 73 — Providers and deployers must notify serious incidents to market surveillance authorities. Deadline varies by jurisdiction.
6
Notify your insurer
Contact your insurance broker or directly your insurer within 24 hours. Provide the SBX Guardian audit export as immediate evidence. Your tamper-evident logs significantly strengthen your position. Do NOT admit liability in this communication — let your legal team guide the statement.
SBX Guardian audit export completed and saved
Hash chain integrity verified (should show: valid)
Physical evidence collected and secured
Regulatory notification assessment completed
Insurer notified with initial evidence pack
Witness statements collected (written, signed, dated)
3
Hours 24–48 · Important
Regulatory Notifications
7
File regulatory notifications if required
If your legal team determines regulatory notification is required, file it within the prescribed deadline. Notifications must include: description of the incident, systems involved, data from SBX Guardian logs, immediate actions taken, and preliminary assessment of cause. SBX Guardian's AI can generate a draft notification report — review with legal before submitting.
EU AI Act Art. 73(2) — Deployers must report serious incidents to providers and market surveillance authorities. Timelines vary: typically 15 days for serious incidents, 3 days for incidents involving death.
8
Initial communication — internal and external
Prepare a brief factual statement for employees. If media inquiries, all responses go through legal and communications lead only. Do not speculate on cause. Acknowledge the incident, confirm investigation is underway, and communicate commitment to safety.
SAY
"We are conducting a thorough investigation and are cooperating fully with all relevant authorities."
DO NOT SAY
"It was a malfunction / operator error / freak accident" — never speculate on cause before investigation concludes.
4
Hours 48–72 · Recovery
Investigation & Evidence Compilation
9
Technical investigation
Use SBX Guardian's AI Analyst to reconstruct the timeline. Ask the fleet chat: "What happened with [system name] in the 2 hours before the incident?" The AI will analyze your tamper-evident logs and produce a reconstruction. Share with your technical expert and legal team — this is powerful evidence in your favor.
10
Root cause analysis
With all evidence collected, conduct formal root cause analysis. Document: what happened, why it happened (technical root cause), contributing factors, and what is being done to prevent recurrence. This document becomes the foundation of your regulatory response and your defense if litigation follows.
11
Return to operation plan
Before restarting: conduct safety review, implement identified corrective actions, obtain sign-off from safety officer, verify SBX Guardian logging is active and hash chain continues from incident. Document the restart decision and who authorized it.
Technical investigation report drafted
Root cause identified and documented
Corrective actions defined with owners and dates
Regulatory notifications filed (if required)
Insurer provided full evidence package
Board/management briefed with factual report
Return to operation plan approved and documented
5
Day 3–30 · Long-term
Post-Incident Compliance Strengthening
12
Update your FRIA and technical documentation
Every incident requires a review and update of your Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment and Annex IV technical documentation. SBX Guardian's FRIA wizard guides this. Updated documentation must be stored and verifiable — SBX Guardian's hash chain covers this automatically.
EU AI Act Art. 72 — Post-market monitoring must include review following serious incidents. Updated FRIA required.
